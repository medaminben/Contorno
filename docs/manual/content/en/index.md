Contorno Indicator measures the surface of a flat object from one photo taken straight down: a hide, a skin, a piece of leather, or any other object lying on a table. It finds the exact outline of the object, draws it over the photo, and shows the surface inside it on a green readout, in square centimetres or square metres.

The outline is found by an artificial intelligence model (IS-Net) that separates the object from the table, pixel by pixel. The surface is then worked out from the number of pixels inside the outline and the size of one pixel on the table, which follows from the camera: its distance to the table, its focal length and its sensor.

## Where to start

Read [Getting started](getting-started) once, then set the camera in [Set Up](set-up). A measurement is three steps: lay the hide flat, take the photo, `Import` it. See [Measure a hide](measure).
